Day1Day, effective November 28, 2026

An app that turns the sleep diary you write each night into a tree.

It is not a diagnosis or treatment and makes no medical promises.

By installing and using the app, you agree to these terms. Apps downloaded from the App Store are also subject to Apple’s standard license agreement, and apps downloaded from Google Play are also subject to the Google Play Terms of Service. If the Korean and English versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

2. What the app does

  • It records the time when you tap to sleep in the evening and tap “I’m up” in the morning.
  • When you pick how last night felt, it draws that night as a tree.
  • As your nights add up, animals visit your forest, and new field guide entries and lands open.
  • Once enough records build up, it shows patterns in your sleep and one thing to try next.

3. Accounts and backup

  • You can use the app without signing in. Your records then stay only on this phone and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google and turn on backup to keep your records in your account and continue on other devices with the same account.
  • You can delete your account in the app’s Settings at any time. Deleted records cannot be restored. If you cannot use the app, follow Delete your account.
  • Please do not share an account or sign in with someone else’s account.

4. Health and medical information

The trees, animals and sentences in the app come from the sleep diary you write. They may differ from how long or how well you actually slept.

The app does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders, makes no promise that your sleep will improve, and does not replace medical advice. If you have trouble sleeping for more than two weeks, talk to a sleep specialist.
