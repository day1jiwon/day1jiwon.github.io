Tap before bed, pick when you wake

It works like the sleep diary used in sleep clinics.

  1. Tap to sleep

    The seed you picked goes in the soil. No sound, just a short vibration.

  2. Pick how last night felt

    Tap “I’m up” and answer one question at a time.

  3. Watch it grow

    The seed grows with each answer. Pick the last one and your tree is done.

  • How was falling asleep?Right away, Tossed a little, Lay awake a long time
  • Do you remember waking up?No, Once or twice, Often, Awake for a long while
  • How deeply did you sleep?Very deeply, Deeply, Okay, Lightly, Barely
  • Do you remember a dream?Vividly, Faintly, Not at all
  • How does your body feel?Refreshed, Fine, A bit heavy, Very tired

Every night grows a different tree

Even the same seed grows differently depending on your morning answers.

  • The deeper you sleep, the thicker the trunk and roots.
  • Vivid dreams bring more blossoms and new shoots.
  • Each time you woke leaves a silver breeze around the tree.
  • A refreshed morning turns the leaves golden.
  • A night you lay awake lets the roots settle later.
  • The longer you sleep, the taller the tree.

There are no scores. A night you woke often still grows a tree, just a different shape.

Questions

Does it track my sleep automatically?

No. You tap when you go to bed and when you get up, then pick how last night felt. It is a sleep diary, so no watch or other device is needed.

How long does the morning take?

About 20 seconds. Tap one answer per question and you’re done.

I forgot to tap before sleeping.

Use “Add last night” on the Today screen and start from the time you fell asleep. That night still becomes a tree.

Will my tree wither if I skip a few days?

No. Trees never wither, and there are no streaks to break. Tap to sleep again and you pick up that night.

Are there scores or rankings?

No. You’re never compared with anyone else.

Does daytime sleep count?

Yes. Shift work sleep and naps become a tree too.

Do I need an account?

No. At first everything stays on this phone. Sign in with Apple or Google and turn on backup, and your forest follows you to a new phone.

What happens if I delete the app?

Without backup, your records go with the app and cannot be restored. With backup on, sign in with the same account and your forest comes back.

Is there an Android version?

Yes, an Android app is coming soon. Sign in with the same account and turn on backup, and your records carry over.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete it right in the app’s Settings. If you cannot use the app, see Delete your account.
