Sleepseed Privacy Policy

Day1Day, effective November 28, 2026

Your records stay on this device and go to your account only when you turn on backup.

If you do not sign in, your records exist only on this device. If you sign in with Apple or Google and turn on backup, the same records are also kept on your account’s server so your forest carries over when you change phones.

No ads and no tracking. We do not sell or hand over your records to anyone.

Where records are stored

Your sleep diary, tree and animal records, and settings are stored on this device. If you sign in and turn on backup, the same records are also kept on your account’s server.

What we back up

  • Account Your account number and email. We receive only what Apple or Google provides, and we do not receive your name or photo.
  • Sleep diary The answers you pick in the morning (falling asleep, waking in the night, how deeply you slept, dreams, how your body feels on waking), and the times you fell asleep and woke up.
  • Forest Your tree, land and animal records, and sleep experiments.
  • Settings Your seed and notification settings.

We do not send photos or location. Because sleep records can be health related, they are sent to the server only when you turn on backup yourself.

How we use it

Only to keep your forest going when you change phones. We do not show ads or track you, and we do not sell or hand over your records to anyone.

Who processes it

  • Supabase Inc. Runs the backup server, which is located in Seoul, Republic of Korea (AWS ap-northeast-2).
  • Apple, Google Confirm it’s you when you use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, and give the app your account number and email.

Using it outside Korea

  • If you turn on backup outside the Republic of Korea, your records are transferred to and stored on the server in Seoul, Republic of Korea.
  • The transfer happens when your records sync after backup is on, and they are sent encrypted over the internet.
  • Turning on backup means you agree to this. Nothing more is transferred after you turn backup off or delete your account.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleted records are removed from the server right away, and copies left in server backups are gone within 7 days.

Deleting

  • Tap Delete Account in Settings to remove your account and backed up records from the server.
  • You can sign out or delete your account in the app’s Settings at any time.
  • If you never signed in, your records exist only on this device and are deleted along with the app.
  • If you cannot use the app, request deletion by email as described in Delete your account.

Widgets and Lock Screen

What your Home Screen widgets and Lock Screen show is shared only within this device, and no account information is included.

Sharing

We send photos or videos only when you tap Share, and only to the place you choose.

Notifications

We schedule notifications on this device. You can turn them off in Settings.

Children

  • Sleepseed is not a kids category app. It is a family app that a parent or guardian can use together with a child on the guardian’s account.
  • In Korea, children under 14 need the consent of a parent or legal guardian to create an account and turn on backup.
  • Children under 13 (in line with COPPA in the United States) must not create an account. Without an account, records stay only on the device and are not sent to the server.

About health information

The sleep diary records how you felt. Sleepseed does not diagnose or treat anything and makes no medical promises.
